帮助

  1. 点「选择 PDF」，在文件中一次选择多份 PDF。
  2. 拖手柄移动整份文件，拖缩略图移动一页。点页面可旋转或移除；需要时可撤销。
  3. 点「合并」或「生成 PDF」，然后点「保存或分享」。原件保持不变。
打不开某份文件？
选择未加密、允许复制的普通 PDF。云端文件不可用时先下载到本机。签名或表单可能丢失，默认不合并。
怎么使用别人发来的材料顺序？
在「导入模板」粘贴口令，或打开 .pdfmergetemplate 文件，再为各槽位选自己的 PDF。
试用结束后呢？
基础合并、排页和导出仍免费。旧文件和模板仍可用；在设置中可以买断高级功能或恢复购买。不自动扣费。

隐私政策

我们不收集个人数据。PDF、草稿和模板在设备上处理并保存，不上传到开发者服务器。App 无账号、广告或统计 SDK。你主动选用的文件提供方、分享目的地及 Apple App Store 按各自政策处理数据。可在设置清理未使用的副本；主动删除某份生成记录不会影响原件。

Help

  1. Tap Choose PDFs and select several documents from Files.
  2. Drag a file handle to move a whole file, or a thumbnail to move one page. Tap a page to rotate or remove it. Use Undo if needed.
  3. Tap Merge or Create PDF, then Save or share. Your originals stay intact.
Can’t open a file?
Choose an ordinary, unencrypted PDF that allows copying. For unavailable cloud files, download a local copy first. Signatures or forms may be lost; these files aren’t merged by default.
How do I use a shared order?
Paste a code in Import template or open a .pdfmergetemplate file, then choose your own PDF for each slot.
What happens after the trial?
Basic merge, reorder and export stay free. Existing files and templates remain available. Use Settings to buy the advanced features once or restore purchases. There’s no automatic charge.

Privacy Policy

We do not collect personal data. PDFs, drafts and templates are processed and stored on your device, with no upload to developer servers. The app has no accounts, ads or analytics SDKs. File providers and share destinations you choose, and the Apple App Store, process data under their own policies. Clean unused copies in Settings; deleting a generated copy does not delete the originals.
